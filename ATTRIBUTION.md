# Attribution

**Slop Slayers' first skill was inspired by [Maggie Appleton's post](https://x.com/Mappletons/status/2114779610596675824)** about guiding AI agents to work at the level of interface nouns, verbs, and grammar before selecting components. Thank you, Maggie, for sharing the idea and references publicly.

The conceptual interaction design skill independently combines and operationalizes ideas from an established body of design and software research. In particular:

- **Daniel Jackson** — *The Essence of Software* (software concepts, state, actions, operational principles)
- **Daniel Rosenberg** — Semantic Interaction Design (conceptual grammar and object–action relationships)
- **Jeff Johnson and Austin Henderson** — *Conceptual Models* (conceptual models before widgets)
- **Sophia Prater** — Object-Oriented UX / ORCA (objects, relationships, calls to action, attributes)
- **Ben Shneiderman** — direct manipulation (visibility, rapid and reversible actions)
- **Don Norman** — *The Design of Everyday Things* (affordances and signifiers)
- **Alan Cooper and collaborators** — *About Face* (application posture)
- **Google Material Design** — environment and spatial hierarchy
- **Apple** — *Designing Fluid Interfaces* and *Meet Liquid Glass* (fluid motion and layered controls)

See the [source map](skills/conceptual-interaction-design/references/source-map.md) for original links, the evidence reviewed, and outstanding research gaps. This repository contains original agent instructions and templates, not copies of the cited books, papers, talks, or illustrations. Attribution does not imply endorsement or collaboration by any of the above authors.
