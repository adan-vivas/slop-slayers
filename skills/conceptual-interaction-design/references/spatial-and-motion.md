# Spatial and motion choices are secondary

**Sources:** [Material Design 1: Environment](https://m1.material.io/material-design/environment.html), [Apple WWDC18: Designing Fluid Interfaces](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2018/803/), [Apple WWDC25: Meet Liquid Glass](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/219/).

These references discuss rendering and interaction principles. They do **not** mandate particular aesthetics for concept-first design.

## Apply only when the model benefits

- Spatial layout: Does proximity/containment encode a meaningful conceptual relationship, or is it decorative?
- Elevation: Does depth communicate hierarchy, navigation, or temporary interaction state? Could a flat treatment be clearer?
- Motion: Does it preserve object continuity between states? Does it respond to redirection/interruption? Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings.
- Control layer: Does separating controls from content help people focus without hiding necessary actions?
- Responsive access: What happens on small screens, keyboard navigation, screen readers, and low-motion settings?

Reject motion and 3D solely because a graph looks interesting. Prioritize meaning, predictability, legibility, and user control.
