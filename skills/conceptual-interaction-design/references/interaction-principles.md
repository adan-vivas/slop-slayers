# Interactions after the model — Shneiderman, Norman, Cooper

**Primary open source:** [Ben Shneiderman, Direct Manipulation (1983)](https://www.cs.umd.edu/~ben/papers/Shneiderman1983Direct.pdf). Other sources are book references with limited preview: [Norman, *The Design of Everyday Things*](https://jnd.org/books/the-design-of-everyday-things-1st-ed/), [Cooper et al., *About Face*](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/About+Face%3A+The+Essentials+of+Interaction+Design%2C+4th+Edition-p-9781118766576).

## Review questions

**Visible objects (Shneiderman):** Is the affected object available in context? Can a user manipulate it directly, or is an indirect command more efficient? Provide incremental feedback; make reversal inexpensive where possible. Do not assume drag-and-drop is always accessible or preferable.

**Signifiers and affordances (Norman):** What actions are physically/technically possible and what cues make them discoverable? Does the interaction communicate the effect, current state, and recovery? Offer keyboard and assistive-technology equivalents.

**Application posture (Cooper):** Is the application a sustained environment that merits persistent UI, or a transient companion that should disappear quickly? Does the app's surface area match the duration and attentional cost of the task?
