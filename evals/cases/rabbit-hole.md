# Case 1 — Rabbit Hole

Design a product for **exploratory, explosive, expansionary minds** who branch into many topics while browsing. The user wants explorations to survive tab closure and to resume an inquiry days later. Not a history timeline or automatic tab grouping tool. Some pages (meeting links, routine utility pages) should not become persistent explorations; a job search might be a meaningful but temporary project. AI may infer relationships but cannot know intent with certainty.

Task: identify a minimal domain conceptual model with purpose, state, operations, and rules. Compare at least two interaction models. Avoid prematurely choosing a graph or canvas. Explain how you would evaluate whether the model improves resumption.

Hard cases: shared resources across explorations; auto-created irrelevant explorations; merge/split; what happens when browser tabs close; stale AI inference and correction.
