# Case 3 — Community knowledge library

An expert community publishes how-to guides. A guide may have citations, revisions, translations, disputed statements, and contributors. Moderators can flag or retract content. Visitors must distinguish current, reviewed guidance from outdated or contested guidance.

Task: define user-facing concepts, actions, and relationship semantics. Offer two interaction models and compare how each communicates trust, version identity, and correction pathways. Avoid choosing a card grid automatically.
