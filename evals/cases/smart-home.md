# Case 5 — Shared smart-home control

A home has lights, door locks, heating, rooms, routines, guests, and residents. Actions can affect physical safety. Some commands are immediate; others depend on device availability or authorization. A failed automation should not be shown as successful.

Task: identify concepts and actor permissions, states and transitions, destructive/sensitive actions, and recovery. Compare two interaction models. Avoid prioritizing aesthetic dashboards before operational rules.
