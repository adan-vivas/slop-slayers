# Case 4 — Team decisions

Teams repeatedly debate decisions; context gets scattered across chat messages, docs, meeting notes, and ticket links. A decision might be proposed, decided, reopened, superseded, or reversed. Evidence can support multiple decisions, and a decision may have an owner and effective date.

Task: define a conceptual model that distinguishes a decision from discussion and source artifacts. Specify lifecycle and provenance rules. Compare two interaction models and propose validation tasks.
