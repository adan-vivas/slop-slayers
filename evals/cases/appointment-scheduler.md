# Case 2 — Appointment scheduler

A community clinic wants appointment scheduling for patients, reception staff, and practitioners. Appointments may be requested, held temporarily, confirmed, rescheduled, canceled, or marked no-show. Two patients might attempt the last opening concurrently. A practitioner can become unavailable. Avoid designing a generic calendar UI first.

Task: model the domain and compare two interaction models appropriate for different actors. Explain state transitions, booking conflicts, cancellation consequences, access control, and undo/recovery.
